Nursing Prep Pro

Semester-wise notes, practice MCQs and previous-year questions for nursing students.

Nursing Prep Pro is an educational Android app. It follows the nursing curriculum semester by semester, with teacher-style notes to study from and questions to practise before your exams.

Get it on Google Play Privacy policy

Free to download. Contains ads. Some content may require an in-app purchase through Google Play.

Features

Semester-wise curriculum
The nursing curriculum laid out semester by semester.
Subject and chapter organisation
Each semester is divided into subjects, and each subject into chapters.
Topic-wise notes
Teacher-style study notes for each topic.
Practice MCQs
Chapter-wise multiple-choice questions to practise with.
Previous-year questions
Questions from earlier exams to work through.
Practice and exam modes
Solve questions at your own pace, or test yourself with timed, exam-style practice.
Revision-focused learning
Content written with revision in mind.

How the content is organised

Everything in the app sits in the same four-level structure, so you always know where you are in the curriculum.

  1. SemesterStart with the semester you are studying.
  2. SubjectPick one of that semester’s subjects.
  3. ChapterOpen a chapter to see its topics and its practice MCQs.
  4. TopicRead the study notes for that topic.

Educational sources and references

Curriculum references may include publicly available resources and syllabus information published by the Indian Nursing Council (INC).

The INC is the body that publishes nursing syllabi and regulations in India. Its documents are linked here so that you can check the original source yourself. The notes and questions in the app are written for study and are not copies of INC publications.
